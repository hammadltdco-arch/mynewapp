Privacy policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026. Loop Chat is operated by NovaPulse Digital LLC. Questions or requests: novapulsedigitalllc@gmail.com.

What we process

To provide accounts and private messaging, we process your email address, user ID, username, display name, optional biography, messages, uploaded photos, conversation memberships, read timestamps, block lists, abuse reports, and optional notification tokens. Your username, display name, and biography can be found by other signed-in users through username search. We do not upload your phone contacts or collect precise location, microphone recordings, advertising IDs, or payment details.

How data is used and shared

We use this information to authenticate users, deliver messages, protect accounts, investigate reports, and respond to support requests. Messages and photos are accessible to conversation participants. Authorized operators and infrastructure providers can access data where necessary for service operation, security, and moderation. This version is not end-to-end encrypted. We do not sell your personal information or use advertising or analytics SDKs.

Service providers and notifications

Supabase provides account authentication, database, photo storage, and server functions. If enabled for this release and permitted by you, Google Firebase Cloud Messaging processes device notification tokens and delivers generic new-message alerts without message text. Infrastructure providers may process network identifiers such as IP addresses, authentication events, and operational logs. Providers may process data outside your country, depending on the operator’s selected hosting region and their infrastructure.

Storage and security

Network connections use HTTPS. Database access is restricted to authorized users using access policies. Android login sessions are encrypted with a key stored in Android Keystore, and app backup is disabled. These measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security. Shared photos are re-encoded before upload to remove embedded metadata. A recipient can still save or screenshot content.

Retention and deletion

Account data and authored messages remain until deleted by the user or removed by an operator. You can delete individual messages or delete your account in Settings. Successful account deletion removes the authentication account, profile, authored messages, uploaded photos, memberships, block records, reports you submitted, and notification tokens from the live service. Messages sent by other people belong to those senders and are not deleted. Copies already saved by recipients cannot be recalled. Provider backups and security logs may persist until their normal rotation; the operator will explain applicable retention when responding to a request. A partially failed deletion can be retried or escalated to support.

Your choices

Edit your profile, block users, delete your messages or account, and allow or deny notifications. You can request access, correction, or deletion by emailing support. We may verify account ownership before acting. See account deletion for the external request process.

Children and safety

Loop Chat is not intended for children under 13, or below the minimum age required in your jurisdiction. If you are under the age of legal majority, you need permission from a parent or guardian. Contact support if you believe a child’s information has been provided inappropriately. See our child safety standards.

Changes

We will update this page when practices change and provide additional notice where required.
