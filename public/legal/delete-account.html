Delete your Loop Chat account

Loop Chat · NovaPulse Digital LLC

Delete inside the app

  1. Sign in to Loop Chat.
  2. Open Settings → Delete account.
  3. Confirm your password, type DELETE, and confirm permanent deletion.

Request deletion without the app

Email novapulsedigitalllc@gmail.com with the subject “Loop Chat account deletion request”, preferably from the email used for your account. Include your username, if known. Do not send your password. We will verify ownership before acting and reply with the status of your request.

What is deleted

Your account, profile, authored messages, uploaded photos, conversation memberships, block records, submitted reports, and notification tokens are removed from the live service. Messages authored by other users remain theirs. Recipient copies cannot be recalled. Provider backups and operational security logs may remain until normal rotation or longer if legally required. See the privacy policy. Deletion is irreversible. If in-app deletion fails, retry or contact support.
