Child safety standards

Loop Chat · NovaPulse Digital LLC · Effective October 26, 2026

We prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation, including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexual solicitation of minors, trafficking, and content facilitating harm to children. These rules apply to all accounts and conversations.

Report a concern

Use Report user in conversation details, or report a specific message using its options. Select Child safety concern. You may also contact our designated safety contact at novapulsedigitalllc@gmail.com. Provide usernames and relevant context, but do not email illegal imagery. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Response and enforcement

The operator reviews safety reports, restricts offending accounts, removes prohibited content, and reports confirmed CSAM to the relevant regional authorities or reporting organizations as required by law. Necessary evidence is handled with restricted access and appropriate legal safeguards. We do not permit retaliation against people who report concerns.

Age requirements

Loop Chat is not intended for users under 13 or below the minimum age required by local law. A parent or guardian may contact the safety address about a child’s account or deletion request.
