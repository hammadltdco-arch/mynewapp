## Full description

A little hello can go a long way.

Loop Chat gives your everyday conversations a calm, welcoming home. Find people by username and stay connected through simple one-to-one messaging—without sharing your phone number or uploading your contacts.

• Make it yours with a username, display name, and short bio.
• Send text messages and share photos in private conversations.
• Pick up where you left off across signed-in devices.
• Block unwanted contacts and report messages or accounts.
• Delete your own messages or your account from Settings.

Loop Chat has no ads. Connections use encrypted transport; this version does not provide end-to-end encryption. Voice/video calls and group chats are not included in this release.

A confirmed email account and an internet connection are required. Loop Chat is not intended for children under 13 or below the minimum age required where they live.
